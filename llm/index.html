How to Train Your LLM

Frontier language models like Fable 5 are trained on trillions of words, over months, at a cost of billions of dollars. We talk to them every day, and yet how they are made remains a mystery to almost everyone who uses them.

None of it is programming. Nobody writes rules for grammar, or facts, or conversation — instead, a simple game is played against a mountain of text, billions of times over, and everything the model can do falls out of playing that game well. The game itself is simple enough to run in a browser tab. What the frontier adds is mostly scale.

Over this series, you'll train a language model of your own, the same way, doing every step yourself. It begins with you playing the game.
