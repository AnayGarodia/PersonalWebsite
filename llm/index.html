How to Train Your LLM

Frontier language models like Fable 5 are trained on trillions of words, over months, at a cost of billions of dollars. We talk to them every day, and yet how they are made remains a mystery to almost everyone who uses them.

A language model is not programmed. It begins as billions of random numbers, and it is trained: show it some text, have it guess what comes next, and nudge the numbers after every miss. Repeat this on enough text, enough times, and the guessing gets so good that conversation comes out of it. That procedure is the subject of this series.

Over the series you will train a model of your own, in this browser tab, doing every step yourself. It starts with you playing the guessing game.
